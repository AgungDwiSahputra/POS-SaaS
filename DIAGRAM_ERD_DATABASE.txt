================================================================================
                          CARDINALITY EXAMPLES
================================================================================

1. USER MANAGEMENT:
   - 1 User can have 1 Tenant (users.tenant_id → tenants.id)
   - 1 User can have many Roles (model_has_roles)
   - 1 Role can have many Permissions (role_has_permissions)

2. PRODUCT MANAGEMENT:
   - 1 Product belongs to 1 Category (products.product_category_id → product_categories.id)
   - 1 Product belongs to 1 Brand (products.brand_id → brands.id)
   - 1 Product can have many Sale Items (sale_items.product_id → products.id)

3. SALES TRANSACTIONS:
   - 1 Sale can have many Sale Items (sale_items.sale_id → sales.id)
   - 1 Sale belongs to 1 Customer (sales.customer_id → customers.id)
   - 1 Sale belongs to 1 Warehouse (sales.warehouse_id → warehouses.id)

4. INVENTORY MANAGEMENT:
   - 1 Warehouse can have many Products Stock (manage_stocks.warehouse_id → warehouses.id)
   - 1 Product can have Stock in many Warehouses (manage_stocks.product_id → products.id)
   - 1 Transfer moves Products from 1 Warehouse to another Warehouse

5. FINANCIAL TRANSACTIONS:
   - 1 Sale can have many Payments (sales_payments.sale_id → sales.id)
   - 1 Expense belongs to 1 Expense Category (expenses.expense_category_id → expense_categories.id)
   - 1 Payment uses 1 Payment Method (sales_payments.payment_type → payment_methods.id)
